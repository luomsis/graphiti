Tesla, Inc. is an American electric vehicle and clean energy company based in Austin, Texas. Tesla was founded in 2003 by Martin Eberhard and Marc Tarpenning, although current CEO Elon Musk joined as chairman in 2004 and later became CEO in 2008.

Tesla designs and manufactures electric vehicles including the Model S, Model 3, Model X, and Model Y. The company also produces solar panels, solar roof tiles, and battery energy storage systems like the Powerwall and Megapack.

In 2022, Tesla acquired SolarCity, a solar energy company founded by Elon Musk's cousins. This acquisition helped Tesla expand its clean energy product line. Tesla's Gigafactory in Nevada produces batteries in partnership with Panasonic.

SpaceX, another company founded by Elon Musk in 2002, focuses on aerospace manufacturing and space transportation. SpaceX developed the Falcon 9 and Starship rockets, and operates the Starlink satellite internet constellation. SpaceX has worked with NASA on multiple missions, including the Crew Dragon program.

In 2022, Elon Musk acquired Twitter for 44 billion dollars, renaming it to X in 2023. This acquisition was controversial and led to significant changes in the platform's content moderation policies.
